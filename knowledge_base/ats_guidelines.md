# ATS Guidelines
Use standard section headings such as Skills, Experience, Education, and Projects. Prefer readable text, consistent dates, and simple formatting. Match terminology from the target job only when it accurately describes the candidate's evidence. Avoid tables, columns, images, and decorative headers when ATS parsing is important.

Keyword coverage should be interpreted with context. A keyword is useful when supported by a truthful achievement or responsibility, not when inserted without evidence.
